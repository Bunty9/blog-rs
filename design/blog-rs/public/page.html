About

blog-rs is the engine behind this site, and an excuse to write about the parts of software that don’t usually get a changelog: the allocator, the scheduler, the syscall you didn’t know you were making.

It’s built the way the writing recommends — server-rendered askama templates over htmx, no SPA framework, assets embedded at build time. The whole thing is fast because there’s very little of it.

What you’ll find here

Long-form, benchmarked posts about systems and performance, mostly in Rust. Code blocks you can run, charts with real numbers, and the occasional dead end documented honestly.

Colophon

Set in Inter and JetBrains Mono. Light and dark themes follow your system by default. There are no trackers; analytics, if any, are first-party and aggregate.
